Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Motivate Me mobile app handles and why. In short: the app needs no account, stores your progress only on your device, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

No Account Needed

Motivate Me does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or other contact details.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your streak, favorite quotes, goals, wins, challenge progress, and settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

AI Coach

When you chat with the AI Coach, the messages in that conversation are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a reply. They are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising. Please do not share sensitive personal information in the chat. When the AI Coach is offline, replies are generated on your device and nothing is sent.

Content Downloads

The app downloads quotes from ZenQuotes and background photos from Unsplash. These requests contain no personal data, but like any web request they reveal your IP address to those services.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries.

Support Emails

If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data. Contact us at ayoubboutglay318@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be communicated by updating the Last Updated date.
