Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install MotivateMe?

Download MotivateMe for free from the Apple App Store. Search for "MotivateMe" and tap to install. The app will appear on your home screen.

Does MotivateMe cost money?

MotivateMe is free to download with optional in-app purchases and subscriptions.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings or Account, and tap "Restore Purchases." Sign in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I manage my subscription?

All subscriptions are managed through Apple ID settings: Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > MotivateMe. You can change your plan or cancel there.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes all refunds. Visit Apple's support page to request a refund.

What data does MotivateMe collect?

See our Privacy Policy for details. We collect minimal personal information and focus on providing a secure experience.

Troubleshooting

App Won't Open or Crashes

  1. Force close the app
  2. Restart your device
  3. Make sure you have the latest version
  4. Check available storage
  5. Try deleting and reinstalling the app

Sync Issues

  1. Verify you're using the same Apple ID
  2. Check your internet connection
  3. Force close and reopen the app
  4. Restart your device

Features Not Working

  1. Update to the latest version
  2. Restart the app
  3. If the problem persists, contact support
