Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install MotivateMe?

Download MotivateMe for free from the Apple App Store. Search for "MotivateMe" and tap to install. The app will appear on your home screen.

Does MotivateMe cost money?

No. Motivate Me is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your phone automatically.

Will I lose my streak if I change phones?

Your streak, favorites, and goals are stored only on your device, so they do not transfer to a new phone, and deleting the app removes them.

What data does MotivateMe collect?

Almost none. There are no accounts, analytics, or ads. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Troubleshooting

App Won't Open or Crashes

  1. Force close the app
  2. Restart your device
  3. Make sure you have the latest version
  4. Check available storage
  5. As a last resort, delete and reinstall the app (note: this erases your streak, favorites, and goals)

Not Getting Notifications

  1. In the app, open Settings and check that Daily motivation is switched on
  2. On your iPhone, open Settings > Notifications > Motivate Me and allow notifications
  3. Open the app once; this refreshes the upcoming reminders

Features Not Working

  1. Update to the latest version
  2. Restart the app
  3. If the problem persists, contact support
