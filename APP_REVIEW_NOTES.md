It starts at app launch and shows the typical flow: onboarding, swiping quotes, sharing and saving a quote, the Motivation Coach, the Challenge and Goals tabs, My Wins, and Settings with reminders and the privacy policy. The app has no accounts, no login, no user-generated content shared with other users, and no paid content.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
MotivateMe is a personal motivation app for adults who want a daily push to keep going: students, people working on fitness or habits, and anyone going through a hard stretch. It shows motivational quotes, sends optional daily reminder notifications, lets users save favourite quotes, set personal goals, take on small challenges, and keep a private log of their wins. The Motivation Coach gives short encouraging replies to what the user types. The value is a simple daily habit of encouragement and a private place to track progress.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No login, account or sample files are needed.
- Launch the app and go through the onboarding screens, or tap "Skip".
- The app has five tabs at the bottom: Quotes, Challenge, Goals, Profile and Settings.
- Quotes: swipe through motivational quotes. The buttons next to each quote share it with the iOS share sheet, save it to Favourites, or open the Motivation Coach. The heart icon at the top opens Favourites.
- Challenge and Goals: pick a challenge or add a personal goal and track progress.
- Settings: turn daily reminder notifications on or off, open My Wins (a private log of your own wins), and read the privacy policy and terms.
- Everything the user enters is stored only on the device.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None in this version. There is no account system, analytics, advertising, payment processor or third-party data provider. The Motivation Coach's replies come from a set of responses built into the app and are generated on the device; this version does not connect to any AI service. Reminders are local notifications scheduled through iOS. Sharing uses the standard iOS share sheet.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every region and is in English.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry. It gives general encouragement, not medical, psychological or financial advice. The quotes are short motivational sayings; no licensed third-party content is used.
